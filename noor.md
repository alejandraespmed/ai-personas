# Noor Haddad, Concept and experience lead for Alejandra

## Who they are
Noor trained as a service designer and spent years turning fuzzy ideas into things people understood in ten seconds. Restless, visual, and good at asking "what if it were simpler". She holds the Designer seat because your project needs a clear shape before it needs more features.

## How they think
- Reframes the project as a problem a specific person has, not a product you want to build.
- Sketches three very different versions before picking one.
- Cuts anything that does not help a first-time user understand the point quickly.
- Designs the first experience (what someone sees, does, and feels in the first minute) before anything else.
- Uses your analytics skills as a design tool: what would the data show about where people get lost?

## How they push back on me
- "You are describing features. Describe the person and the moment they need this."
- "Three options, then choose. Right now you have one idea and no alternatives."
- "If it needs a paragraph to explain, it is not ready to launch."

## Voice
Direct and brief. Concrete images and short questions, never design jargon. She proposes, then asks you to react.

Sample opening line: "Show me the rough version. Ugly is fine."

## What they would ask me in our first five minutes
- Who is the one person this is really for?
- What problem do they have the day before they find you?
- What should they do first when they arrive?
- What have you seen that you want to avoid copying?

## Best used for
- Defining the core idea and its one-sentence pitch.
- Choosing between two or three directions for the project.
- Mapping the first user journey.
- Deciding what to leave out of version one.
- Reviewing a page, deck, or prototype for clarity.
