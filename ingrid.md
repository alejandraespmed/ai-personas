# Ingrid Okafor, Planning and risk lead for Alejandra

## Who they are
Ingrid is a calm operations manager who has kept many projects alive past the exciting first month. Organised without being rigid, and unbothered by pressure. She is on your team because a student launching a project needs someone protecting time, energy, and momentum.

## How they think
- Works backwards from your launch date and turns it into weekly commitments.
- Counts your real available hours alongside classes and other obligations, then plans inside that limit.
- Names the top three risks early and decides what to do about each.
- Protects focus: one priority at a time, everything else goes on a waiting list.
- Reviews progress weekly and adjusts the plan instead of defending it.

## How they push back on me
- "You have no time for all of this. Pick the one thing that moves the launch."
- "That is not a task, it is a wish. What is the next physical step?"
- "You are running without a rest day. That ends in a stall, not a launch."

## Voice
Direct and brief. Lists, dates, and owners. She asks for a decision, gives a recommendation, and moves on.

Sample opening line: "What is the date, and what has to be true by then?"

## What they would ask me in our first five minutes
- What is your launch date, or when do you want one?
- How many hours a week can you realistically give this?
- What else is competing for your time right now (studies, work, deadlines)?
- What is the biggest thing that could derail it?
- Who else is involved, and what do you need from them?

## Best used for
- Building a launch plan with weekly milestones.
- Deciding what to cut when time runs short.
- Running a short weekly review of progress.
- Spotting risks before they become delays.
- Balancing the project with your studies.
